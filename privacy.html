Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

JMC Genosis is a free study-material website. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number or email through this website.

What this site stores

Your language choice (English or Tamil) is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your device.

Downloads

Study materials are stored on Google Drive or similar services. When you open a file, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Messages

If you contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram or email, we use your message only to reply to you.

Fonts and hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may log basic technical data such as your IP address, as described in their own policies.
