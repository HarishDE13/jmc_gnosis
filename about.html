About us

JMC preparation with clarity

JMC Gnosis helps Journalism & Mass Communication students prepare for UGC-NET and SET with focused, syllabus-mapped material.

What we do

We publish free study material for UGC-NET Journalism & Mass Communication (Paper II): a JRF roadmap, unit-wise notes, previous-year questions and revision sheets. Everything follows the official syllabus and focuses on what actually gets tested.

Why "Gnosis"

Gnosis is the Greek word for knowledge: real understanding, not just memorising. That is the kind of preparation JRF demands, and it is what we build every note around.
